# survey-design

Design a short survey that produces usable data.

> Demo skill: an example to show how the library looks with many skills. Not published in the npm package.

## When to use

When you need survey questions with answer formats. Give it the **goal**: what decision the survey informs.

## Process

1. Ask only questions that change the decision.
2. Use balanced scales and neutral wording.
3. Pilot with three people.

## Output

Survey questions with answer formats.

## What to avoid

- Double-barreled questions.
