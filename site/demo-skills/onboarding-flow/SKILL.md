# onboarding-flow

Design a first-run flow that gets new users to their first success quickly.

> Demo skill: an example to show how the library looks with many skills. Not published in the npm package.

## When to use

When you need a step list with screen goals and copy. Give it the **product**: what the product does and who it's for.

## Process

1. Define the first meaningful success.
2. Cut every step that doesn't lead there.
3. Defer setup until it is needed.

## Output

A step list with screen goals and copy.

## What to avoid

- Tours that explain the interface instead of doing the task.
