# research-synthesis

Turn interview notes into themes, insights and opportunities.

> Demo skill: an example to show how the library looks with many skills. Not published in the npm package.

## When to use

When you need themes with quotes and opportunity statements. Give it the **notes**: raw notes or transcripts.

## Process

1. Tag observations, not interpretations.
2. Cluster tags into themes.
3. Write insights as tensions with evidence.

## Output

Themes with quotes and opportunity statements.

## What to avoid

- Counting mentions as proof of importance.
