# competitor-brief

Summarize a competitor's positioning, pricing and weak spots.

> Demo skill: an example to show how the library looks with many skills. Not published in the npm package.

## When to use

When you need a one-page brief. Give it the **competitor**: the competitor's name or site.

## Process

1. Collect their positioning and pricing.
2. List what customers praise and complain about.
3. Find the gaps you can own.

## Output

A one-page brief.

## What to avoid

- Copying their messaging.
