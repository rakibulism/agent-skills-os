# email-sequence

Write a short onboarding or nurture email sequence.

> Demo skill: an example to show how the library looks with many skills. Not published in the npm package.

## When to use

When you need three to five emails with subjects and timing. Give it the **goal**: what the sequence should achieve.

## Process

1. Map each email to one step toward the goal.
2. Write subject lines under 50 characters.
3. Give each email a single call to action.

## Output

Three to five emails with subjects and timing.

## What to avoid

- Several calls to action in one email.
