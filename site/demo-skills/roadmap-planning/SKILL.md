# roadmap-planning

Turn a list of ideas into a prioritized, outcome-based roadmap.

> Demo skill: an example to show how the library looks with many skills. Not published in the npm package.

## When to use

When you need a roadmap table with rationale. Give it the **ideas**: candidate features and goals.

## Process

1. Group ideas by the outcome they move.
2. Score by impact, confidence and effort.
3. Lay out Now, Next and Later.

## Output

A roadmap table with rationale.

## What to avoid

- Dates on everything far ahead.
